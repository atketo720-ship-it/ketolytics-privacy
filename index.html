Ketolytics Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Ketolytics is a nutrition and lifestyle tracker for Android. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Data we collect

Most information stays on your phone. We do not create a user account.

  • First name, age, height, and weight you enter during setup
  • Food log, carb targets, meal plans, grocery list, and weight entries
  • Optional Health Connect data (weight, steps, active calories) only if you turn that tab on
  • Camera access only to scan a barcode. We do not keep a photo library

How we use it

Only to run the app: welcome you by name, track nutrition and weight, build a meal plan, and optionally sync with Health Connect. We do not sell data. We do not use ads. We do not use your data for marketing lists.

Food search

When you search foods or look up a barcode, the app may send the search text or barcode to USDA FoodData Central over HTTPS so nutrition facts can be shown. Check the product label. Packaged foods change.

Sharing

We do not share your log with other companies. If you enable Health Connect, weight or activity may sync with Health Connect on your device under Google’s Health Connect rules.

Retention and deletion

Data lives on your device. Uninstall Ketolytics or clear the app’s storage in Android Settings to delete it. We do not keep a copy on our servers.

Children

Ketolytics is for adults 18 and over.

Contact

Questions: use the email listed on the Ketolytics Play Store page.
